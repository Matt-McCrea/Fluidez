## Rules as applied

- **Order.** SEQ's positions 0 to 536 (382 existing lessons, 17 new, 28 verb days, 110 practice, writing and review days). B1 verb sets are placed by meaning (model.ts B1_VERB_SETS): b1-n26 imaginar, ocupar; b1-n27 formar; b1-n31 aprobar; b1-n36 mejorar; b1-n38 participar; b1-n39 adelantar.
- **Latin American correction.** SEQ's units a1-n03 (perfecto, "Today and ever", 33 to 37) and a1-n06 (indefinido and the state imperfects, "What happened", 60 to 65) trade places whole, so the preterite takes the perfecto's slot and the perfecto lands where SEQ had the preterite, 27 SEQ positions after its prerequisite g:preterite-ser-ir-hacer (on this spine: preterito day 53, gr-preterito-perfecto-a2 day 102). This also removes a problem SEQ itself flagged: the perfecto now comes after gustar (38) and reflexives (52), so *me he levantado* and *me ha gustado* are available. The stubs gr-perfecto-vs-indefinido-a2, task-a2-hoy-ayer and gr-pasados-repaso-b1 are retitled to the Latin American split (preterite for anything finished, perfecto for experience and "not yet"). Vosotros and Spain's "today" perfecto leave A1 in the node model (g:vosotros-recognition off presente and gr-pronombre-sujeto-a1, g:spain-pasts off the perfecto lesson) and go to a new B2 recognition stub, gr-vosotros-espana-b2, in "Reading the news".
- **Week one.** a1-u01 and a1-u02 are copied from data/course.js as source text (comments, `passage` and `write` fields, dlg-presentarse and gr-cognados-a1 included). They consume 14 of SEQ's lessons and both irregular verb days; gr-sonidos-a1 stays the off-path reference card. What SEQ's first unit has left (fn-no-entiendo-a1, gender-articles) opens the next unit.
- **Weeks.** Every other unit is a week: Introduce (the grammar point, else the big lesson), Drill, Input (one, or two in a six-session week), Output (the task, else output practice), Mixed review with the unit check. Each SEQ unit is cut into consecutive runs of one to three lessons and verb days, one run per week, with the fewest weeks (then the fewest four-session weeks and the fewest moves): the order inside a SEQ unit changes only where a grammar lesson moves to the front of its week. A week may hold a second grammar point; it goes last, just before the Output, so the Drill or an Input sits between the two and the Output or Review day practises it a day or two later. Weeks do not cross SEQ units, so each keeps its theme; SEQ's task lessons end the run before them and are that week's Output. A verb day is always an Input and never leads.
- **Drills.** 53 Drill days reuse a SEQ practice, writing or review day at most 2 positions after the Introduce; 89 are inserted. Output practice: 14 reused, 71 inserted. Reviews: 38 reused, 104 inserted. SEQ non-lesson days not reused: 4.
- **Merges** are 94 aliases in tools/reseq/out/lesson-aliases.proposed.js; 2 SEQ merges are not applied (gr-acentuacion-a1: kept as the longer accent lesson behind the gr-sonidos-a1 reference card (4 November); dc-entonacion-a1: a day of frozen a1-u02 (week one, 4 November)). In the node model a merged lesson's teaches move to its target.
- **Splits and new lessons** are path days with `stub: true` (20, listed below). In the node model each split part takes the nodes SEQ names for it (model.ts NODE_MOVES).
- **Make optional.** 123 lessons go to deep units with `status: 'reference'` where they are not already reference lessons: the twelve deep units the course has, with SEQ's re-homings, plus 15 new ones (the B1-B2 vocabulary track as four themed units and the C1 track as seven, so neither is one shelf of seventy). nt-textura-consistencia-b1 and fn-recordar-olvidar-c1 stay on the path (phase 0 ruling): nt-textura-consistencia-b1 in b1-n39, fn-recordar-olvidar-c1 in c1-n56.
