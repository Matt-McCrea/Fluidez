# The original app, mapped (4 November 2026)

Read-only reconnaissance of the root app on branch `next`. `node tools/validate-content.js` passed at the time (194,029 checks).

## 1. Architecture

No bundler, no modules: `index.html:34-92` loads about 60 deferred scripts that attach `window.*` globals, in the order perf, data files (taxonomy, connectors, rubrics, verbs, vocab, idioms, grammar-docs, grammar, passages, apply, writing, topics, strand-lessons, course, resources), core (`engine.js`, `lessons.js` which builds `GRAMMAR_LESSONS` at load, `checker.js`, `srs.js`, `profile.js`, `curriculum.js`), the five stage views in `js/views/`, feature screens, games, then `session.js`, `onboarding.js`, `shell.js`, `app.js`, `pwa.js`.

**Shell** (`js/shell.js:24`): six persistent tabs, each mounted once: `inicio` (`renderInicio`, shell.js:61), `jugar` (`Games.renderTab`), `practicar` (`Practice.render`), `progreso` (`Progress.render`), `palabras` (`UserWords.render`), `mas` (`showMasMenu`, shell.js:306). Overlays (`Shell.openOverlay/closeOverlay`) swap the tab bar for `#stage-host`; `js/app.js:19` (`App.go`) routes `session`, `errors`, `games`, `home`. Más rows: Cómo funciona (`Onboarding.run`), Gramática (`Grammar.render`), Profundizar (`DeepDive`), Escribir (`WriteSpace`, a journal), Recursos, Ajustes, a level switcher.

**Profiles are CEFR bands** (`js/profile.js:49-88`, from `data/taxonomy.js:31`): A1, A2, B1, B2, C1. Old names survive as aliases (beginner A1, standard B1, refresher B2). Only A1 and A2 have `usesCurriculum: true`. Level resolves from `?p=`, then `fluidez.profile`, then A1. `README.md` is stale (old trio, a streak).

**localStorage** (`js/settings.js:19-38`): `fluidez.progress` (`studied{lessonId:1}`, `beginnerDay`, `courseSig`, `read{passageId:day}`, `history`, `lastDay`, `total`, `streak`, `recall`); `fluidez.srs` and `fluidez.srsSchema` ('3'); `fluidez.errors`, `fluidez.vetoed`, `fluidez.accepted`; `fluidez.profile`, `caps`, `theme`, `voice`, `focus`, `suggest`, `onboarded`; `fluidez.userWords`, `journal`, `captured`; `fluidez.games`, `gamePrefs`, `arcade`, `study`; `fluidez.liga`, `liga.outbox`.

**PWA:** `sw.js` is cache-first; `CACHE_VERSION` hashes `ASSETS`, regenerated by `tools/bump-cache.js`; `ASSETS` must mirror `index.html` by hand and the validator checks it. `juegos/` has its own worker. The app loads about 7.5 MB (`strand-lessons.js` 3.4 MB, `game-index.js` 2.3 MB, `passages.js` 1.8 MB, `vocab.js` 0.8 MB).

## 2. The daily session (`js/session.js`)

Modes (session.js:20): `rapido` review only (cap 10), `corto` review plus 4 apply, `diaria` and `larga` all five stages. Stages are `StageReview`, `StageLearn`, `StageComprehend`, `StageApply`, `StageProduce` in `js/views/`.

Determinism is not date-based (the header comment is stale): `pickFocus` (session.js:320) takes today's focus from progress; item sampling is seeded by lesson id only the first time a lesson is seen, otherwise `Math.random`.

A1 and A2 walk `Curriculum.seq()` with the counter `prog.beginnerDay`; `courseSig` (session.js:271) fingerprints the sequence and rebuilds the counter from `studied` when the course changes; a catch-up loop steps over days already studied; day types are grammar lessons, `verbs` days and `practice` days (`learn.js:367-380`). B1 and above take the first unstudied lesson from `Curriculum.lessonsFor(cefr)`; `dayIndex` is the lesson's position in `COURSE_DAYS`.

**Gating:** passages, apply items and writing tasks are filtered by `level <= level`, then by taught tense via `taughtTenses` (session.js:257) and `tenseSchedule` (session.js:221); the "floor" is the present, never gated; `tenseReached(tk, dayIndex, studied)` (session.js:244); an emptied pool falls back to the unfiltered one; `safeVerbs` and `safeWriting` at A1; the optional `Focus.narrow` theme.

**Passage choice:** aligned (`passageUsesFocus`: concept, tense, or 3 lesson words via `LexMatch`), then same theme, then any; a chosen Focus outranks the tiers; unread first (`prog.read`), full pool when all read; a day's authored `passage` in `data/course.js` overrides (session.js:608); `richest` (session.js:668) picks most unmet vocabulary per word.

**Produce** by `produceStyle`: `guided` (A2) build, translate, write, paragraph; `build`/`full` build, translate, 2 writes, paragraph from level 2; essay day at `level>=6 && dayIndex%7==0` (session.js:497).

**Studied** is written when Learn finishes (`runStage`, session.js:891; A1/A2 `beginnerDay++`), not at session end; the passage is marked read when Comprehend finishes. **End of session** (`renderComplete`, session.js:926): `bumpStreak`, `Focus.tick`, `Liga.sessionDone`, results, a Suggest card, a tense-game card, the copy-to-Matthew `Report` modal. "Do it again" calls `start(mode)` on the advanced counter.

**Stopping mid-session:** `idx` and `ctx` are module variables; nothing is persisted; a reload restarts from Review; graded cards stay graded; quitting after Learn loses the rest of the day.

## 3. SRS (`js/srs.js`)

Leitner: `INTERVALS=[0,1,2,4,8,16,32,64,120,180]` (srs.js:47); good goes up a box, a miss resets to 0. Graduation at box 8 with 4 right in a row (`dominado`, spot-checked 5%). Leeches: 6 lifetime lapses or 3 misses in a row; cleared by 2 right. `batch(pool, maxDue, maxNew, shuffleFresh, freshPrefer)` (srs.js:186) picks most overdue weakest first; overflow rescheduled 1 to 3 days. Ids: `v:<es>:meaning` (and `:gender`), `vm:<inf>`, `vt:<inf>:<tense>`, `i:<es>`, `k:<es>` phrases, `ac:<focus>` contrasts, `p:` and `g:<tense>:<person>` recall, `err:*`. Caps per band: due 20, 22, 20, 24, 28; new 7, 8, 5, 6, 8. Feed (`review.js:33`): vocab gated by `Profile.wordAllowed`, idioms, grammar recall and probes with `srs !== false`, verb meanings and `vt:` pairs from cloze items gated by tense, contrasts and phrases, error cards. Learn enrols recall and the first 6 phrases (`learn.js:340`). Typed or choice (`resolve`, review.js:148): authored mcq always choice; A1 always ES to EN choice; A2 graduated (choice while box <= 1, then typed EN to ES); B1 and up typed EN to ES; distractors are three glosses of the same kind; typed grading is `Checker.checkExact` with meaning alternatives.

## 4. Lessons

`js/lessons.js:465` `build()`: tense lessons generated by `tenseLesson` (lessons.js:143) from `data/grammar-docs.js` with engine tables; concept lessons from `data/grammar.js`; strand lessons from `data/strand-lessons.js`; `MERGES` (lessons.js:498) fuses 142 lessons by concatenation, old ids in `LESSON_MERGED_INTO`; `withRecall` turns `probes` into recall cards and `reproducible()` allows at most 8 words (lessons.js:375). Strand schema: `id, strand, cefr, level, theme, pcic[], title, canDo, moment, summary, keywords[{es,en}], sections[{h,html}], exponents[{es,en,register,note}], contrasts, pitfalls, examples, probes (mcq, cloze, recall)`; genre lessons use `moves/model/checklist`.

Counts: `ALL_LESSONS` 674; `GRAMMAR_LESSONS` 573 = lesson days in `COURSE_DAYS` (663 days, 75 units); 101 shelved. By prefix on the path: gr 71, fn 173, nt 166, dc 33, gn 52, task 63, legacy tense/concept 14, dlg 1. By band: A1 63, A2 65, B1 141, B2 128, C1 162. A1 spans 107 days, A2 81, B1 165, B2 139, C1 171; 28 verb days, 62 practice days. Average section words: A1 145, A2 130, B1 78, B2 137, C1 176.

Sample defects: `gr-articulo-indefinido-a1` puts a figurative proper-noun aside at A1; `nt-necesidad-obligacion-b1` lists *tener que, hay que, deber* in its keyword table and teaches none of them in the body.

## 8. Hub and navigation

Inicio: status strip, today's card (unit, day N of M, Focus row), start, skip, other durations, QuickPlay card, tense-check and game cards, "Practise your mistakes", next-up and suggestion boxes. Practicar: mode chooser (smart review, by theme, grammar, weak spots, next step), quick drills (typed conjugation, flashcards), a daily write-about prompt. Progreso: "What you can now do", per-unit status with Marcar and Ponme a prueba (`UnitCheck`), per-strand lesson lists, SRS boxes, heatmap, weak spots; every lesson openable via `LessonRun`. Palabras: own words, bulk add, tags. Settings: export/import JSON, start over, review cap, perf. Onboarding: three cards, a nine-question binary-search placement over lesson mcq probes. **No dictionary, verb-table or search**; conjugation tabs only inside tense lessons (`learn.js:251`); Resources links out.

## 9. Games

`js/games.js:81`: Traducción, Vocabulario, Conjugación, Gramática, Racha, Emparejar, Supervivencia, Uno u otro; plumbing in `gamescore.js`, `gameitems.js`, `gameround.js`; items carry SRS ids. `quickplay.js` recommends. `arcade.js` and `juegos/` are a separate games PWA on `data/game-index.js`. `liga.js` is the weekly translation league, the only networked feature (Supabase, `server/league.sql`), offline-first with an outbox.
