# Juegos refocus (6 Nov 2026)

Goal: a fun, effective way to learn Spanish on a commute. Fewer games, three parts done
properly, then build game types back up from there.

Priorities, in Matthew's order: **verbs in sentences** and **vocabulary grinding** first,
short translation (common phrases) a distant third.

## Decisions

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Shelf | One big **Jugar** plus three tiles: **Verbos**, **Palabras**, **Frases**. Every other game is hidden, its code kept. |
| Jugar mix | 45% verbs in sentences, 40% words, 15% phrases; misses and due cards come back first. |
| Round shape | Each tile offers both: **Tranquilo** (10 questions, no clock) and **Contrarreloj** (60 s, pauses when the phone locks or the app is hidden). |
| Input | Tap first (4 options); once an item has been answered right it comes back typed. |
| Words | Most common words, verbs and phrases first, from a public frequency list (subtitle-based, openly licensed), with well filled-out themes. Long-term promise: a long-time player knows all the most relevant Spanish. |
| Verb tenses | Default: all tenses at the player's level. Per-round picker to choose one or more, including untaught ones. |
| Weekly check | Once a week a level check pops up, tests each tense, and offers the next tense the player does not know. |
| Next tense | A short tense card inside Juegos (form, 3 examples, when it is used), then a practice round locked to that tense; a link opens the full lesson in Fluidez. |
| Verb sentences | Generated in batches (LatAm/Colombian Spanish, 10 words or fewer, one verb gapped) and passed through the content gate. Nothing generated in the app. |
| Frases | Most frequent everyday phrases and short sentences, English to Spanish; typed at 6 words or fewer, chips up to 8. |
| Progress | Coverage meter ("742 of the 2,000 most common words", per theme and for verbs) and the games keep feeding the shared spaced-repetition memory. |

## Stages

1. **Shelf and rounds** (code only, existing content): the new board, Tranquilo and
   Contrarreloj, pause on hide, tap-then-type, Verbos from the existing 580 verb-gap
   sentences with a tense picker, Jugar's mix, Frases capped short.
2. **Frequency**: import the list, rank words, verbs and phrases, list the common words
   the app is missing, fill out the themes (through the gate).
3. **Content**: verb-gap sentences for the top verbs across the main tenses; the common
   phrases list. Batch generated, gated.
4. **Progress and checking**: coverage meter, weekly level check, tense cards.

## Measured before the refocus (5 Nov)

Gramática was 36% quiz questions about the language and 21% el/la gender; Conjugación
was 100% typed meaning to form with "(subjunctive)" labels; from B1, Traducción was 85%
chip sentences of about 8 words. Nothing paused when the phone locked.
