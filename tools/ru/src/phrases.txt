# Svobodno phrases, written by hand. One per line:
#   id | band | theme | English | Russian | note (optional) | also accepted (optional, ; separated)
# Stress: an apostrophe after the stressed vowel of every word with two vowels or more (ё needs none).
# ты is informal (a friend, a child), вы is polite or more than one person.
